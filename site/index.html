About BPI Commercial LLC

A Family Legacy in Southeast Texas Real Estate

BPI Commercial LLC provides full-service commercial real estate brokerage and property management across Southeast Texas. With roots dating to 1976, our business has evolved across two generations while remaining guided by one consistent principle: the client’s best interest comes first.

Our Story

From Burns Real Estate to BPI Commercial

Our story began in 1976, when Robert S. Burns founded Burns Real Estate and Management Company. Through a commitment to truth, fairness, and personal service, he established a respected name in Southeast Texas commercial real estate. As the business grew, it became Hare, Burns & Osborne Real Estate-Commercial.

In 1990, Robert’s son, Andrew R. Burns, joined the firm. In 1992, father and son reorganized the company as Burns Properties, Inc., continuing to build on the relationships, experience, and values established by Robert.

Today, Andrew carries that legacy forward through BPI Commercial LLC, bringing decades of brokerage and property management experience to every engagement. He personally manages each client relationship and transaction, providing consistent guidance from the first conversation through closing.

Clients benefit from both Andrew’s hands-on experience and the knowledge developed throughout the company’s history. His active membership in the National Association of Realtors, Texas Association of Realtors, and Houston Association of Realtors further supports his commitment to professional service.

Referrals and repeat clients have sustained the business across generations—a reflection of the trust earned through honest advice, hard work, and lasting relationships. We invite you to get to know our company and the people behind it.

Brokerage Services

Commercial Real Estate Services for Owners, Investors & Tenants

BPI Commercial LLC provides full-spectrum brokerage services — from property disposition and investment sales to site selection and lease negotiation.

01

Seller Representation

We help commercial property owners market, position, and sell their properties with precision. Our seller representation process begins with a thorough property analysis, competitive pricing strategy, and a targeted marketing campaign designed to reach qualified buyers — not just generate showings.

  • Property valuation and pricing strategy
  • Targeted buyer outreach and marketing
  • Offer analysis and negotiation
  • Due diligence and closing coordination
  • Exchange and disposition planning
02

Buyer Representation

We help buyers and investors identify, evaluate, and acquire commercial properties that align with their investment or operational strategy. We provide comprehensive market analysis, property comparables, and negotiation support to ensure our clients make informed acquisitions.

  • Market and submarket analysis
  • Property identification and screening
  • Financial analysis and underwriting support
  • LOI preparation and purchase negotiation
  • Due diligence coordination
03

Landlord Representation

We help commercial landlords maximize occupancy and lease value for their properties. From repositioning a vacant space to structuring long-term leases with creditworthy tenants, our landlord representation services are designed to protect and grow your asset's performance.

  • Leasing strategy and pricing
  • Property marketing and tenant outreach
  • Tenant screening and qualification
  • Lease negotiation and structuring
  • Renewal and expansion management
04

Tenant Representation

We represent businesses in identifying, comparing, and securing commercial space on favorable terms. Our tenant representation services provide the market intelligence and negotiating leverage businesses need to make sound real estate decisions.

  • Site selection and space search
  • Market surveys and space comparisons
  • Lease term analysis and negotiation
  • Tenant improvement allowance negotiation
  • Renewal, relocation, and expansion advisory
05

Investment Sales

We help clients evaluate, acquire, and dispose of income-producing commercial properties. Our investment sales practice serves private investors, family offices, and institutional buyers looking to transact on commercial real estate with confidence.

  • Cap rate and cash-on-cash analysis
  • Income property marketing and disposition
  • Investor buyer identification and outreach
  • Redevelopment and value-add opportunity analysis
  • 1031 exchange coordination
06

Land & Development Brokerage

We advise buyers, sellers, and developers in land transactions for commercial development. Whether you're selling a development site or sourcing land for a project, our brokerage provides the market data and representation you need to transact with confidence.

  • Land valuation and highest-use analysis
  • Zoning and entitlement research
  • Developer outreach and disposition marketing
  • Land acquisition and site selection
  • Ground lease structuring

Our Representation Services

How We Represent Clients Across Commercial Real Estate

Our brokerage exists to represent clients — not just facilitate transactions. Every engagement is built around your goals, your timeline, and your definition of a successful outcome.

🏷️

Selling a Commercial Property

We represent property owners in the sale of commercial real estate. Our seller representation process includes property analysis, strategic pricing, targeted marketing, buyer qualification, and negotiation — from listing through closing.

We don't just list your property. We position it, market it to qualified buyers, and guide you through the transaction with clear communication and expert counsel at every step.

🔑

Leasing a Commercial Property

We represent landlords seeking to lease commercial space to qualified tenants. Our landlord representation services cover leasing strategy, property marketing, tenant outreach, lease negotiation, and occupancy optimization.

Whether you have a single vacant suite or an entire commercial building, we help you attract tenants, structure the right lease, and protect your investment over the long term.

📍

Finding Space for a Business

We represent tenants in the search for commercial space — from site selection through lease execution. Our tenant representation services give businesses the market data, site comparisons, and negotiating leverage they need to secure the right space on favorable terms.

We work exclusively for the tenant's interests — not the landlord's — ensuring you have an informed advocate at the negotiating table.

📊

Buying Investment Property

We represent buyers and investors in the acquisition of commercial investment properties. From income-producing retail centers to multifamily assets, we help our clients identify, evaluate, and acquire properties that match their investment strategy.

Our buyer representation includes market analysis, financial underwriting, LOI preparation, and full negotiation support through closing.

🌐

Land & Development Opportunities

We represent buyers, sellers, and developers in land transactions for commercial development. Whether you're selling a development site, seeking land for a project, or evaluating ground lease opportunities, we provide the market intelligence and representation to move forward with confidence.

Our land brokerage services include zoning review, highest-use analysis, developer outreach, and transaction structuring.
